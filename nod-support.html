NOD Support

NOD by 37°N — Sleep, habits and personal experiments

Get help

Email support@37degreesn.com or open Feedback & Support in NOD. Include your app version, phone model, and a description of the problem. Please do not send passwords or health records.

Sign-in and access

Use the same sign-in method and account you used before to restore your history. NOD currently uses invitation-based access for new accounts. Contact support if you need help with your invitation.

Health records and sync

NOD can read sleep, heart rate, resting heart rate and HRV from Apple Health or Health Connect with your permission. It does not write to either health store. Available records depend on your wearable, its companion app, and your chosen permissions. Check that records appear in Apple Health or Health Connect, then use NOD's Vitals screen to sync again.

Backup and recovery

Open Account → Backup & recovery to check the status of your account-backed encryption keys. Sign in to the same account after changing phones. A successful key backup does not confirm that every record has synced.

Experiments and check-ins

NOD helps you record observations and compare habits over time. Results depend on the data you provide and may be inconclusive. NOD is for personal tracking and learning, not diagnosis or treatment.

Privacy and account deletion

Read the NOD privacy policy. To delete your shared 37°N account and associated data, use Account → Delete Account & All Data in the app or follow the web deletion instructions. Account deletion affects your connected 37°N apps.
